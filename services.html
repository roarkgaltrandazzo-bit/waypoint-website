Services

Practical advancement support for the parts of funding work that usually get skipped.

Waypoint helps private schools, nonprofits, and faith-based organizations build the systems behind sustainable funding: donor communication, stewardship, campaign planning, board support, event follow-up, and simple tracking.

Assessment

Advancement Assessment

Find the weak spots before the next campaign exposes them. We review your current rhythm across communication, campaigns, stewardship, board participation, event follow-up, and tracking.

System design

Advancement System Design

Build the calendar, donor touchpoints, stewardship rhythm, board roles, reporting cadence, and ownership structure your team can actually run.

Campaigns

Campaign and Appeal Support

Plan the ask before the deadline starts making decisions. We support annual giving, year-end campaigns, special appeals, sponsorships, and events.

Stewardship

Donor Stewardship

Keep donors connected after they give through thank-you rhythms, impact updates, sponsor reports, donor letters, and recurring touchpoints.

Enablement

Board and Staff Support

Clarify advancement chair responsibilities, relationship champion roles, thank-you participation, sponsor introductions, and reporting rhythm.

Events

Gala and Event Follow-Up

Turn events into relationship-building opportunities through attendee follow-up, sponsor stewardship, donor segmentation, and post-event communication.

1

3

5

Implementation

6

Handoff

The goal is to leave your organization with a system it can keep running.
