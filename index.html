Advancement Framework

Advancement connects cultivation, solicitation, and stewardship.

Funding is not only the ask. It depends on what happens before, during, and after the ask.

Communication, introductions, sponsor awareness, mission storytelling, and trust-building before the ask.

Appeals, sponsorship requests, campaign conversations, event asks, annual giving, and major gift invitations.

After the Gift

Stewardship

Thank-yous, updates, impact reports, personal follow-up, and continued care after the gift.

Questions

Common questions before the first conversation.

Is Waypoint a consultant or an outsourced advancement department?

Waypoint is an advancement partner. We help plan, structure, write, build, track, and support the funding work, but we do not replace your donor connections or make solicitation calls on your behalf.

Do you create campaign and donor materials?

Yes. Waypoint can help create appeal language, sponsorship materials, donor updates, impact reports, stewardship messages, board talking points, follow-up templates, and planning tools.

Do you work with the board?

Yes. Board advancement structure is often part of the work. That may include defining the advancement chair role, committee support, board participation expectations, relationship champion roles, and reporting rhythms.

Do you help with accountability?

Yes. Waypoint helps create simple tracking and reporting rhythms so leaders can see open next steps, follow-up status, campaign activity, board participation, and stewardship commitments.

Do you solicit gifts directly?

No. Your people keep the trust and make the asks. Waypoint helps prepare, equip, and support them with structure, messaging, materials, and follow-through.

Who should be part of the first conversation?

Usually the executive director, head of school, advancement leader, development director, board president, board treasurer, advancement chair, or whoever is closest to the funding work.

Is this only for schools?

No. Waypoint works with nonprofits, private schools, and mission-driven organizations that need stronger support around funding, campaigns, stewardship, board participation, sponsorships, and follow-up.

How is Waypoint different from a general nonprofit consultant?

Waypoint focuses on the advancement function behind funding. We support the planning, materials, board structure, stewardship, campaign rhythm, sponsor follow-up, and tracking that help funding work move across the year.
